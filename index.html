Lucent Pack - Translucent Icons — Privacy Policy

Lucent Pack - Translucent Icons is an offline icon pack by TechiBoy Studios. This policy explains what the app uses on your device and what happens when you choose to share an icon request.

1. Privacy by design

Lucent Pack does not require an account. It contains no advertising, analytics SDKs, advertising identifiers or background usage tracking. It does not sell your information or build a profile of how you use other apps.

2. Installed app information

When you open Lucent Pack, it reads launcher-visible app names, package identifiers and launcher activity names. This information is used locally to match icons, show coverage and identify missing logos. It does not read the content of those apps, your messages, contacts, files or browsing history.

3. Local settings and storage

Onboarding progress, language, appearance, favourites, request drafts and request history are saved on your device. The icon catalogue and artwork are bundled with the app. Installed-app information is processed locally. A preview image you choose is used locally; Lucent Pack does not provide an account or cloud-sync service.

4. Icon requests and sharing

A request contains only the selected app names, package identifiers and launcher activity names, plus the Lucent Pack version. Tapping Request icons opens your email app with a draft addressed to techiboystudios@gmail.com. Nothing is sent until you tap Send in your email app. Your email provider processes that message under its own privacy practices. If no email app is available, you can explicitly copy the request and send it yourself. Review the selected apps before sending, especially if their names reveal personal or work information.

5. Permissions and wallpaper

Lucent Pack and its refresh widget do not require runtime permissions or special access for their core functions. They do not use Accessibility, system-settings write access, location, contacts, camera or microphone. Choosing a preview image uses Android’s system picker and grants access only to that image, not your photo library. The image stays on your device; previewing it does not change your wallpaper. Translucent icons do not apply real wallpaper blur.

6. The optional refresh widget

You decide whether to add or remove the widget. Tapping it opens the launcher’s icon-pack picker; you complete the Default → Lucent Pack selections yourself. It does not watch your screen, perform taps or change your phone’s theme automatically. Its appearance can follow your device’s light or dark mode without monitoring other apps.

Icon status, tile and shortcuts

Lucent Pack reads which icon pack your phone has selected and whether it is in light or dark mode, only on your device, so it can tell you when your icons need a refresh. A system job runs only when one of those settings changes, to update the widget. Nothing is sent anywhere. The Quick Settings tile and long-press shortcuts only open screens you choose, including the launcher’s icon-pack picker. They do not change your settings.

7. External apps and support messages

Lucent Pack has no Internet permission and does not automatically upload app lists or settings. If you choose to open a store, browser, email or sharing app, that service handles its own activity. If you email TechiBoy Studios, we use the information you provide to answer your message and assess your request; email providers also process that correspondence.

8. Your choices and retention

You can remove favourites and clear local request history in Lucent Pack. Clear the app’s local data in Android settings or uninstall it to remove saved preferences, drafts and preview-image data. You can remove its widget through your launcher. These actions do not recall messages you already shared or delete records held by a sharing service. Contact us about correspondence you sent us.

Local request limits

Request limits are enforced locally using timestamps and the app identifiers included in opened request-email drafts. Clearing request history does not reset these limits. Opening an email draft counts even if you later cancel it. Clearing Lucent Pack’s data or uninstalling it removes these local records. No quota records are uploaded automatically.

9. Contact and policy updates

For privacy questions or concerns, contact techiboystudios@gmail.com. If the app’s data practices change, this policy will be updated to describe them before the changed version is released. Last updated: 6 November 2026.
